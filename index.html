How It Works

Three Steps to Better Care

Easy to start, easy to keep going — every check becomes part of your cat's health record.

Create a Cat Profile

Add name, breed, and basic info. The more complete the profile, the more accurate AI assessments and assistant advice become.

Take a Photo or Record Audio

Pick a check module. Shoot front-facing, well-lit, clear photos. The meow module supports live recording or picking from your library.

Review Reports & Trends

Get AI insights and care suggestions. Track history on the Health tab. If something looks off, consult a vet — don't wait.
