Five Smart Health Checks

Take a photo or record a meow — AI analyzes and returns a diagnosis with actionable advice. Build your cat's profile and track changes over time for more accurate results.

Pain Check

Upload a face or body photo. AI scores facial expression and posture to detect signs of discomfort — ideal for senior cats who hide pain.

Vomit Check

Snap a photo of vomit. AI identifies color, texture, and possible causes — hairballs, diet issues, or something that needs a vet visit.

Stool Check

Monitor digestive health from stool photos. Track consistency and color changes to spot GI problems early.

Dental Check

Photograph teeth and gums from the side. AI checks for tartar, inflammation, and oral health red flags.

Meow Translation

Record your cat's meow. AI translates the sound, guesses the scenario, and suggests how to respond.

AI results are for reference only — not a substitute for professional veterinary diagnosis. Seek immediate care in emergencies.
