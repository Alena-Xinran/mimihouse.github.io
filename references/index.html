References

NineLivesCat · Clinical & research sources

NineLivesCat health checks are inspired by established veterinary assessment tools and peer-reviewed literature. AI outputs are for reference only and do not replace professional veterinary diagnosis.

Pain Check (FGS)

  • Evangelista MC, Watanabe R, Leung VSY, Monteiro BP, O'Toole E, Pang DSJ, Steagall PV. Facial expressions of pain in cats: the development and validation of a Feline Grimace Scale. Scientific Reports. 2019;9:19128. doi:10.1038/s41598-019-55693-8
  • NineLivesCat methodology note: Facial scoring follows the five FGS action units (ear position, orbital tightening, muzzle tension, whiskers change, head position). NineLivesCat extends this with four additional body-state dimensions to form a 0–18 composite score for app reporting.

Stool Check

  • Hennet P, et al. Fecal scoring systems in veterinary practice (Purina 1–7 scale application in feline studies). Frontiers in Veterinary Science. 2023. doi:10.3389/fvets.2023.1039931
