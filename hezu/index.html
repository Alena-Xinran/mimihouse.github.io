Roommate Cats

An old bookshop, a deadpan manager cat, and a woman who was afraid of cats for twenty years.

Su Man's ex moves out and leaves his cat behind. She's scared of cats and wants to rehome him, until she finds out he has been "going to work" at the old bookshop downstairs every day for seven years.

The two leads

The cats are the stars

Manager · 8 years old

Niangao

Orange and white, with a notch in his left ear. Manager of the Evening Breeze bookshop: in at ten, out at six, paid one can a day. Deadpan, never meows. The show's narration is his inner voice.

(thinking) Got an employee, forgot the cat.

On probation · 3 years old

Guotie

Orange and white, ears intact. She came from a copycat cat café, got a name after being rescued to the old street, and now "commutes" to the bookshop during the day. Meows quietly, and used to sit up straight whenever a camera pointed at her.

mew… (quietly)

Play with them for a bit

  • Tap Niangao to hear what he's thinking (he doesn't meow).
  • Stroke his head back and forth and he purrs.
  • Drag the can to him: that's today's pay.
  • Move close to Guotie and she sits up straight.
  • Niangao works 10:00–18:00 Beijing time. Otherwise he's asleep.
