FAQ

Before you start

Can NineLivesCat diagnose illness?

No. It gives risk flags and keeps health records. A photo cannot confirm a disease, and one low-risk result does not rule anything out. If something keeps looking wrong, contact a vet.

What is the pain score based on?

Facial signs draw on the Feline Grimace Scale (FGS). The 0–18 overall score also includes exploratory body signs, so it is not the original FGS and the extended score has not been clinically validated.

Can the meow feature understand my cat?

It guesses a likely situation and state from the sound. It cannot translate meows into human words. Think of it as a fun way to learn about cat behavior.

Where can I use it, and does it cost anything?

Download NineLivesCat on the App Store. In China, search 「九龄猫」 in WeChat for the free mini program. App subscription options and prices are shown on the App Store and in the app.
