How it works

Less guessing for owners, better records for vets

Connect at discharge

The owner enters the vet's link code in NineLivesCat, or scans the clinic's QR code. No separate sign-up.

Check in with a photo each day

Redness, discharge, appetite: answered by tapping options, plus one photo. About 30 seconds.

AI reviews and color-codes

Comparing today with earlier photos, it flags improving or worsening and gives a first grade. Cases that need attention go to the top.

The vet confirms and replies

The vet decides what's next: reply, ask the owner to come in, or change the grade. Owner questions stay in their own list.

Screens are in Chinese. The service currently runs in China.

Our rule

AI helps. Vets decide.

  • AI can only raise a case's urgency. It never tells a vet "this is fine".
  • Every reply to an owner is confirmed by a vet before it goes out.
  • When a vet overrides the AI, the reason is recorded so the AI keeps getting better.

FAQ

What clinics usually ask

Does AI handle cases instead of the vet?

No. AI organizes the records and flags risk; the vet reviews, adjusts the grade and decides what to do. In an emergency, owners should contact the clinic directly rather than wait for an online reply.

How is it priced?

Clinics join for free. The clinic sets the price of the follow-up service owners buy, and the fee is shared between the clinic and NineLivesCat, with the clinic keeping the larger share. Details are agreed during the pilot.

How much time does it take a vet?

Cases are pre-sorted into needs attention, watch, recovering well and no check-in, with reply drafts ready. Recovering-well cases can be confirmed together, so most days take about ten minutes.

Where is it available?

The owner side runs in the NineLivesCat WeChat mini program, so it currently serves clinics in China, with pilots in Hangzhou. Clinics elsewhere are welcome to write to us.
