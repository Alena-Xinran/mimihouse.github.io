Overview

ZhiLiao ("the App") is a focus and motion-play app for iPhone (with an optional Apple Watch companion). The App has no accounts, no self-hosted backend, and does not collect personally identifiable information. By using the App, you acknowledge this policy.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No registration or sign-in.
  • No upload of your name, email, phone number, photos, or contacts.
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • No transmission of your motion, audio, or usage data to our servers (the App has no backend).

Device Permissions & On-Device Processing

To provide features, the App may request the following system permissions. Related data is processed on your device only, unless otherwise governed by the operating system:

  • Motion & Fitness (gyroscope / accelerometer): Used to drive friction sound from spinning, wind-up focus timing, and idle/"phone pickup" detection. Motion data does not leave your device.
  • Local Network: The duel ("斗蝉") feature uses Apple Multipeer Connectivity for peer-to-peer play with nearby devices. Traffic does not go through our servers; peers exchange only temporary session data such as scores.
  • Notifications: If you enable shoulder/neck stretch reminders, local notifications are scheduled on-device. They are not routed through our servers.

On-Device Storage

The App may store preferences and simple stats locally (e.g. UserDefaults), such as reminder settings, today's focus minutes, and session counts. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear local data.

Audio & Haptics

Friction sounds, cicada white noise, and haptics are generated or played locally. The App does not record or upload microphone ambient audio for cloud processing.

Third Parties & System Services

The App relies on Apple system frameworks (such as Core Motion, Multipeer Connectivity, and User Notifications). Those services are governed by Apple's privacy practices and your device settings. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because there is no account system or backend, we cannot actively determine a user's age.

Your Choices

You may revoke motion, local network, or notification permissions in system settings at any time, or uninstall the App to clear local data. Questions: alena@mimi.house.
