Four ways to play

Best on a real device — gyroscope, haptics, and nearby duel.

01

Wind-up

Spin to set focus time. Cicada white noise while you work; an alert if you idle; a soft summer fade when you’re done.

02

Swing

Gyroscope spin rate drives friction sound in real time, with rope-tension haptics in your hand.

03

Duel

Two nearby iPhones compete — louder and steadier wins. Peer-to-peer, no server.

04

Stretch

Optional local reminders to stand up, swing for two minutes, and loosen your shoulders.

Privacy, simply

No accounts, no cloud sync, no ads or analytics SDKs. Motion and duel data stay on your device.
