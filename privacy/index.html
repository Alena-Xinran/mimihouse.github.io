1. Overview

Welcome to NineLivesCat (the "App" or "we"). The App is provided by Mimi House (mimi.house), including the iOS client and WeChat Mini Program. We value your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide health checks, the smart care assistant, and membership services, we may collect:

  • Account information: WeChat OpenID, Apple ID identifiers, nickname, and avatar (if you authorize them).
  • Cat profiles: Name, breed, age, weight, and other basic details you provide.
  • Check content: Photos you upload (face, vomit, stool, teeth, etc.), audio/video for meow recognition, notes, and context you add.
  • Chat history: Conversations with the AI care assistant to provide a continuous chat experience.
  • Device & logs: Device model, OS version, app version, error logs, and similar data to keep the service stable and secure.
  • Payment information: Subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store or WeChat virtual payments. We do not store your card number; we keep order IDs, subscription status, and other necessary transaction records.
  • Location (optional): Used only with your permission, e.g. to suggest nearby veterinary clinics.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Run AI health check analysis and generate reference insights and care suggestions;
  • Provide meow recognition/translation and smart assistant chat services;
  • Build and maintain cat health profiles and check history trends;
  • Process membership subscriptions, orders, and support requests;
  • Improve features, fix bugs, and maintain system security;
  • Display publicly shared check content (e.g. cat community feed) only with your consent.

4. AI Analysis & Third-Party Services

AI features require sending your uploaded content to our servers (api.mimi.house) for processing. We may use third-party AI models and cloud providers for image, audio, and text analysis. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service and are bound by confidentiality and security obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored in protected server environments. Transmission uses HTTPS encryption.
  • We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or tampering.
  • Check records and chat history are kept until you delete them or close your account, unless law requires otherwise.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your cat profiles and check records;
  • Revoke optional permissions such as location (via system settings);
  • Close your account and request deletion of related personal data;
  • Contact us about privacy: alena@mimi.house.

7. Children

The App is intended primarily for adult pet owners. If you are under 18, please use the App only with a parent or guardian's consent and supervision.

8. Membership & Auto-Renewal

iOS subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store. If auto-renewal is enabled, you can cancel anytime in your App Store account settings. WeChat Mini Program memberships are processed through WeChat payment. Pricing and benefits are as shown in the App.

9. Disclaimer

  • All AI results are for reference only and do not replace professional veterinary diagnosis, examination, or treatment.
  • Facial pain detection is based on the Feline Grimace Scale (FGS). Accuracy depends on photo quality.
  • If your cat shows severe symptoms (difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, loss of consciousness, etc.), seek veterinary care immediately — do not rely on this tool.
  • By using the App, you understand and accept these limitations.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
